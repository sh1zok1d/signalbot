Real RMUT 64.24 min, pseudo 89.39, Δ −25.15 min, 95% −31.42..−19.07, N=1231. Five of five years, DAY, WEEK, both sides, and leave-one-year-out keep the sign.

RMUT over 240 minutes is the integral of the unresolved-survival curve. The 240-minute resolution rate is one point on that curve. #365 already published the rest of the curve on the Binance discovery sample:

- Resolved-by gap about +10.2, +11.9, +12.6, +11.0, and +8.1 pp at 15, 30, 60, 120, and 240 minutes.
- Conditional resolution is higher for the real arm in every predeclared interval, including after 15 minutes.
- BREAK quartiles are faster (median 20 vs 40 min). REJECT quartiles match (median 30 vs 30).
- Aalen–Johansen incidence by 240 minutes: real BREAK 0.470 vs pseudo 0.379; real REJECT 0.438 vs pseudo 0.446.

That pattern matches the #357 decomposition (more BREAK, REJECT not higher, fewer NEITHER) seen as a survival curve rather than as a single terminal rate. The 240-minute Binance gap of +8.1 pp is the same kind of contrast as the BitMEX +8.0 pp. It is not a rescoring of #357.

What the records do not contain is a split of the −25.15 minutes into the part implied by the terminal rate and the part implied by timing among events that resolve. That split was not estimated here. Independence of the clock from the resolution-frequency object is `INDEPENDENCE_UNRESOLVED`. The pair status is likely overlapping. The clock is not given its own Level Engine layer.
