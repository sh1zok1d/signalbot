**Owner practice.**
- A hidden divergence in the trend direction, with the same indicator families (RSI 14/21, MACD default preset, CVD).
- Example (bearish): price makes a lower high while the indicator makes a higher high.
- Stop beyond the previous swing high. Target around the next levels: close 50% there, trail the rest.
- Exit manually if the divergence breaks.
- BTC and ETH perps; 1h, 4h, 1D; since 2024.

**Mechanism.** A pullback inside a trend fails to restore momentum, and the trend resumes. This is a continuation claim, a different mechanism from the reversal after absorption in the absorption seed.
